A canvas (<citem 'polydecorations:canvas'>) is a 16×16 picture you paint one pixel at a time, right where it hangs.

### Image: metacraft:canvas/wall Four canvases side by side, painted as one picture.

### Header: Making one
Put a <citem 'minecraft:paper'> in the middle of a crafting grid and fill the other eight slots with sticks. Right-click any block face to hang it: on a wall, on the floor or on the ceiling.

### Header: Painting
Right-click the hanging canvas with:
- any <bold>dye</bold> to paint that pixel its colour;
- <citem 'minecraft:coal'> to make the pixel darker, <citem 'minecraft:bone_meal'> to make it lighter;
- <citem 'minecraft:paper'> or a <citem 'minecraft:sponge'> to clear it.

### Body:
Hold a <citem 'minecraft:brush'> in your main hand and the dye or tool in your off hand to paint a 3×3 area at once. Canvases hung side by side, facing the same way, paint as one surface. Hit a canvas to take it down; the picture stays on the item.

### Header: Finishing touches
Craft the canvas together with:
- <citem 'minecraft:honeycomb'> to <bold>wax</bold> it, so nobody can paint on it any more;
- a <citem 'minecraft:glow_ink_sac'> to make it <bold>glow</bold>, an <citem 'minecraft:ink_sac'> to undo that;
- <citem 'minecraft:shears'> to <bold>cut</bold> it, so cleared pixels are see-through, <citem 'minecraft:paper'> to undo that;
- a dye to give it a background colour, a <citem 'minecraft:water_bucket'> to wash that out.

### Header: Copying
Craft a painted canvas with one or more empty canvases and you get a copy of the picture for each.
