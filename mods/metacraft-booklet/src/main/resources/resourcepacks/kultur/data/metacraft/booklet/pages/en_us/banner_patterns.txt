### Image: metacraft:beside/kultur/banners The chapters' own patterns go on banners the way vanilla's do: in a <citem 'minecraft:loom'>, in any dye colour, Cerise, Laserviolet and RGB included. Craft the banner onto a shield and the shield shows it too.

### Header: Chapter patterns
Every chapter has a pattern of its own, its logo, and it needs no pattern item. Put a banner and a dye in any loom and pick your chapter from the list.

### Header: The IT clubs and Pirkko
<bold>ITK</bold>, <bold>QMISK</bold>, <bold>TMEIT</bold> and <bold>Pirkko</bold> each need their pattern item in the loom, like the flower or creeper pattern: the <citem 'kultur:itk_banner_pattern'>, <citem 'kultur:qmisk_banner_pattern'>, <citem 'kultur:tmeit_banner_pattern'> or <citem 'kultur:pirkko_banner_pattern'>. The item is not used up. They are in no chest; Punkphöz, a wandering trader who turns up now and then, sells them.

### Header: The painting
Hang a <citem 'minecraft:painting'> on a wall with room for one 3 wide and 4 high and you may get IT's own: <bold>The Guardian of Kistan</bold>, by Emelie Stark.<nl>

<gray>The first four and the painting are IT's. Each chapter's own pattern is its logo.</gray>
