### Image: metacraft:beside/kultur/banners The chapters' own patterns go on banners the way vanilla's do: in a <citem 'minecraft:loom'>, in any dye colour, Cerise, Laserviolet and RGB included. Craft the banner onto a shield and the shield shows it too.

### Header: In any loom
<bold>ITK</bold>, <bold>QMISK</bold> and <bold>TMEIT</bold> need no pattern item. Put a banner and a dye in any loom and pick them from the list.

### Header: Pirkko
The <bold>Pirkko</bold> pattern needs its pattern item, the <citem 'kultur:pirkko_banner_pattern'>, in the loom, like the flower or creeper pattern. The item is not used up. It is in no chest; Punkphöz, the wandering trader, has it now and then.

### Header: Chapter patterns
Every chapter has a pattern of its own, with its own pattern item for the <citem 'minecraft:loom'>. You find them in structure chests, or buy them from Punkphöz, a wandering trader who turns up now and then.

### Header: The painting
Hang a <citem 'minecraft:painting'> on a wall with room for one 3 wide and 4 high and you may get IT's own: <bold>The Guardian of Kistan</bold>, by Emelie Stark.<nl>

<gray>The first four and the painting are IT's. Each chapter's own pattern is its logo.</gray>
