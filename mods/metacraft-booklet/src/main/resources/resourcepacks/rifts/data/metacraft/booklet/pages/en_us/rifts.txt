### Header: The realm is unstable
For now a lit nether portal takes you nowhere. It opens a <bold>rift</bold>: a cracked, flickering tear in the frame, with the nether showing through.<nl2>Light a rift twice more and it tears open. Piglins come through in waves, led by drummers and bombed from chained ghasts. Beat every wave and the rift pays out in gold and glowstone.
