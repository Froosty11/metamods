### Header: The last wave
### Image: metacraft:beside/rifts/general A piglin brute with a red banner on its head and a golden axe. It comes in the last wave of a large rift, with 100 health, twice a brute's. It has a red boss bar of its own.

### Header: Spoils
Killed by a player, it drops:
- its head, a <citem 'minecraft:piglin_head'> named Piglin General;
- a <citem 'minecraft:blaze_rod'>;
- 4 to 8 <citem 'minecraft:quartz'>;
- one time in four, a <citem 'minecraft:piglin_banner_pattern'>.
