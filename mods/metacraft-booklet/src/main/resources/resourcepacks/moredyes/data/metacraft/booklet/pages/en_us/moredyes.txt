Two colours vanilla does not have: <citem 'moredyes:cerise_dye'> and <citem 'moredyes:laserviolet_dye'>. They work like any dye, on almost everything a dye works on.

### Header: Finding them
A <bold>sniffer</bold> digging in grass or dirt sometimes turns up a <citem 'moredyes:cerise_torchflower'> or a <citem 'moredyes:laserviolet_torchflower'>, as often as its own torchflower seeds and pitcher pods. One flower makes one dye; plant the rest. Sniffers hatch from a <citem 'minecraft:sniffer_egg'>, found in the suspicious sand of warm ocean ruins.

### Header: What they colour
- Wool, carpet, beds, candles, banners and shields
- Terracotta, glazed terracotta, concrete and concrete powder
- Stained glass and panes, shulker boxes, bundles
- Wool and concrete stairs and slabs, cushions
- A happy ghast's harness, a llama's carpet
- Sheep: use the dye on one
- Leather and wolf armour and firework stars, mixed with other dyes as usual
- Sign text, in exactly the colour; editing the sign keeps it
- A canvas, in the nearest colour a canvas has
- A pet's collar, in the nearest vanilla colour
