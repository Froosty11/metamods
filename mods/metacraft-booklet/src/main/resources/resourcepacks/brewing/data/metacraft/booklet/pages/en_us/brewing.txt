### Image: metacraft:beside/brewing/barrel Drinks are brewed in three steps, and most need only one or two of them: <bold>cook</bold> the ingredients in a cauldron, <bold>distil</bold> the brew in a brewing stand, and <bold>age</bold> it in a barrel. The closer you keep to a drink's times, the better it comes out.
