### Header: Building one
### Image: metacraft:beside/brewing/barrel A barrel lies on its side, 4 blocks long and 3 by 3 across, all of one wood:
- each slice across is round: <bold>stairs</bold> on the four corners, <bold>planks</bold> on the four sides;
- the two end slices are filled in, with a plank in the middle; the two middle ones are hollow;
- it stands on four <bold>fences</bold>, one under each bottom corner of the end slices.

Then place a <citem 'brewery:barrel_spigot'> on the middle plank of either end. The barrel becomes one block; the spigot is its door.

The spigot is crafted from a stick and a stone over a plank:
- <citem 'minecraft:stick'> <citem 'minecraft:stone'>
- <citem 'minecraft:oak_planks'> (any planks)

### Header: Ageing
Put the mixtures in through the spigot and wait. Ageing goes on in real time, a matter of hours for the best drinks, and keeps going while nobody is near. Some drinks want a particular wood; the recipe says which. Leave a drink in too long and it gets worse again.
